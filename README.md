# Hi, I'm Vijay 👋

### Computational Materials Scientist | Machine Learning | Scientific Computing

I develop **physics-based and machine-learning methods for materials science**, combining atomistic simulations, high-performance computing, and AI to study and predict the behavior of advanced materials.

My work bridges **DFT → Molecular Dynamics → Machine Learning → Multiscale Modeling**, with a particular focus on 2D materials, fracture mechanics, machine-learning interatomic potentials, and graph neural networks.

---

## 🔭 What I'm Working On

- 🔬 **Atomistic Fracture Mechanics** — Mode-I, Mode-II and mixed-mode fracture of 2D materials
- 🤖 **Machine-Learning Interatomic Potentials** — MLIP/MTP, MACE and related approaches
- 🕸️ **Graph Neural Networks** — physics-informed and equivariant GNNs for materials
- ⚛️ **First-Principles Modeling** — DFT calculations of structural, mechanical and electromechanical properties
- 🧩 **Multiscale Modeling** — connecting atomistic simulations with XDEM and phase-field fracture
- 🧠 **Scientific Machine Learning** — physics-informed ML, surrogate modeling and uncertainty-aware prediction
- 🔋 **Energy Materials** — AI-assisted modeling of battery materials and interfaces

## 🚀 Selected Projects

### 🔬 Atomistic Fracture of 2D Materials

LAMMPS + MLIP/MTP framework for atomistic fracture simulations of graphene and related 2D materials.

**Implemented:**
Mode-I · Mode-II · Mixed-Mode · Edge Cracks · Inclined Cracks · Cross Cracks · Kinked Cracks

**Tech:** `LAMMPS` `MLIP/MTP` `Python` `ASE` `OVITO` `HPC`

---

### 🧩 Atomistically Informed XDEM / Phase-Field Fracture

Multiscale framework connecting atomistic mechanical and fracture information with continuum-scale XDEM and phase-field fracture simulations.

**Tech:** `PyTorch` `Scientific ML` `XDEM` `Phase-Field Fracture`

---

### 🤖 Machine-Learning Interatomic Potentials

Development and validation of ML interatomic potentials for mechanical deformation, fracture, structural stability and finite-temperature molecular dynamics.

**Tech:** `VASP` `LAMMPS` `MTP/MLIP` `MACE` `M3GNet`

---

### 🕸️ Graph Neural Networks for Materials

Physics-informed graph neural networks for predicting materials properties from atomic structures and physically meaningful descriptors.

Applications include:

- polarization
- strain-gradient response
- atomic forces
- stress tensors
- mechanical properties

**Tech:** `PyTorch` `PyTorch Geometric` `GNN` `Equivariant ML`
